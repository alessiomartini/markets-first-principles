Markets that never close. Crypto trades continuously; equities do not, and the difference is not cosmetic. A closing auction creates a single official price that index funds, derivatives and performance reporting can all reference, and an overnight gap concentrates the reaction to news into one opening print rather than spreading it across a thin Sunday morning. Continuous markets remove the gap and get, in exchange, a liquidity cycle that follows the clock anyway — volume and depth still collapse when the major financial centres are asleep.
