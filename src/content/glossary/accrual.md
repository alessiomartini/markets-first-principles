---
term: "Accrual"
aliases: ["accrued interest"]
track: 1
status: written
---

Interest that has been earned but not yet paid. Because bonds pay coupons at discrete dates while interest accrues continuously, a buyer between coupon dates compensates the seller for the portion already earned — which is why a bond's *dirty* price (what you pay) exceeds its *clean* price (what is quoted). Forgetting this is one of the most common errors in a first fixed-income calculation.
