---
term: "Additivity"
aliases: ["time additivity", "aggregation"]
track: 3
status: written
physics: "The property that makes a variable a sum of independent increments, and therefore the one every limit theorem is stated about."
---

The property that a quantity measured over a long period equals the sum of the same quantity measured over the sub-periods. **Log return**s have it; **simple return**s do not. This is not a technicality but the reason finance works in logs at all: additivity is what lets central limit theorems, cumulant expansions and scaling arguments apply, and without it none of Track 3's machinery would be available.
