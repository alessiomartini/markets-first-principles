term: "ADR"

A negotiable certificate issued by a US bank representing shares in a foreign company, so that the foreign equity can trade on a US exchange in dollars during US hours. It is a wrapper rather than a different asset, and its price should track the underlying share adjusted for the exchange rate — deviations are a classic, and usually small, arbitrage.
