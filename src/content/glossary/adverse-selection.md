term: "Adverse selection"
aliases: ["toxic selection", "informed trading"]

---

The systematic tendency for the counterparties most eager to trade against your quote to be the ones who know something you do not. It is the fundamental cost of **quoting**: a maker wins a small amount from each uninformed trade and loses a large amount to each informed one, so the **bid-ask spread** must be set to balance the two. Every structural feature of modern market making — quote lifetimes measured in microseconds, cancellation rates above 95% — is a response to adverse selection.
