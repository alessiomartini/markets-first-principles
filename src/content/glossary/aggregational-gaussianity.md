---
term: "Aggregational Gaussianity"
aliases: ["aggregational normality"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The observation that returns measured over longer horizons look more Gaussian than returns measured over short ones: daily returns are strongly **fat tails**, monthly returns much less so, annual returns nearly normal. It is what a central limit theorem predicts, so its presence is unremarkable — what is remarkable is the *rate*, which is far slower than independence would give, and that slowness is direct evidence of dependence between returns.
