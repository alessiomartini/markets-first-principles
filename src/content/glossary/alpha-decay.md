---
term: "Alpha decay"
aliases: ["signal decay", "decay of predictability"]
track: 7
status: written
---

The decline of a signal's predictive power, either as the horizon lengthens (a signal that knows tomorrow but not next month) or in calendar time as the pattern becomes known and traded. McLean and Pontiff (2016) found that published stock return predictors were about a quarter weaker out of sample and more than half weaker after publication.
