---
term: "Alpha"
aliases: ["Jensen's alpha"]
track: 4
status: written
physics: "A residual: what is left of a signal after the explained modes are subtracted, and so a statement about the model as much as about the data."
---

The part of a portfolio's return that is not explained by its exposures to the market (**beta**) and to other chosen factors, estimated as the intercept of a regression. It is defined relative to a model: a different set of factors gives a different alpha, and a positive estimate may be a missing factor, luck or a real edge.
