---
term: "Arbitrage bound"
aliases: ["no-arbitrage bound", "static bound"]
track: 1
status: written
---

A limit on an instrument's price enforced by the existence of a cheaper way to get the same payoff — a call cannot cost more than the underlying, put-call parity ties puts to calls, a bond cannot yield less than a riskless one of the same maturity. These bounds are the most robust results in finance because they need no model of anyone's behaviour, only that free money gets taken.
