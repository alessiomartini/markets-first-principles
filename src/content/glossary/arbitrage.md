A trade that cannot lose: same payoff, lower price. True arbitrage is rare, brief and mostly automated, and the word is used far more loosely on trading floors to mean relative-value positions that are merely *likely* to converge. The distinction matters enormously, because a real arbitrage needs no capital cushion and a "convergence trade" can move against you for longer than your financing lasts.
