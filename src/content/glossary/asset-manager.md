A firm investing money on behalf of others against a stated mandate, typically measured against a benchmark index. The mandate is the important part: a manager benchmarked to an index is not trying to maximise return but to beat a specific comparison, which makes deviating from the index the thing that carries career risk. That structure explains a great deal of otherwise puzzling behaviour, including why enormous sums track indices mechanically and why index inclusion moves prices.
