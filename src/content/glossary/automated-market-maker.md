---
term: "Automated market maker"
aliases: ["AMM", "constant function market maker"]
track: 6
status: written
---

A program that quotes a price for a pair of assets from the contents of a pool, instead of matching orders in an order book. Liquidity providers deposit the assets and earn the traders' fees, and arbitrageurs keep the pool's price in line with prices elsewhere at the providers' expense. The first widely used design, Uniswap in 2018, used the constant product formula.
