aliases: ["ADV"]

---

The mean quantity traded per day over a recent window, and the unit in which order size is universally discussed — a trade is described as "5% of ADV" rather than in shares, because that ratio is what predicts its cost. It is also the denominator in the square-root impact law, which is why an estimate of ADV is the first input to any execution decision.
