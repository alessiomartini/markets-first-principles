A futures curve where longer-dated contracts trade below nearer ones, the opposite of **contango**. It implies a negative net **cost of carry**, which for a physical commodity usually means a *convenience yield*: someone needs the material now badly enough to pay for holding it rather than to be paid. Backwardation is therefore a signal of physical tightness, and it rewards rather than penalises a long roller.
