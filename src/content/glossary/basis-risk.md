---
term: "Basis risk"
aliases: ["basis"]
track: 1
status: written
---

The risk that the thing you hedged with does not move exactly like the thing you were hedging. An airline hedging jet fuel with crude futures is exposed to the crude-to-jet spread; a **standardisation**-driven contract never matches anyone's exact exposure, so basis risk is the residual left behind by using a standard instrument against a specific problem. It is the reason hedging reduces risk rather than removing it, and the reason a hedge that looked perfect can lose money in exactly the crisis it was bought for.
