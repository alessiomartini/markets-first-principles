aliases: ["the basis"]

---

The difference between two prices that ought to be nearly the same: spot against futures, one reference rate against another, a bond against its swap. Basis is where a great deal of institutional risk actually lives, because positions are usually hedged in a closely-related instrument rather than an identical one — and the residual is **basis risk**. Bases are usually small, mean-reverting, and occasionally catastrophic when leveraged.
