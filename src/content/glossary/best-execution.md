The duty of a broker to obtain the best possible result for a client's order. It is not a duty to find the lowest price alone: the broker must weigh price, cost, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size and the nature of the order. It matters because the broker usually chooses where the order goes, and that choice can be influenced by payments from venues, which is the conflict behind the debate on payment for order flow.
