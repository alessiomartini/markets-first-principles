---
term: "Bid-ask spread"
aliases: ["spread", "quoted spread", "the touch"]
track: 2
status: written
---

The distance between the best **bid** and the best **ask**: the round-trip cost of trading a small quantity immediately. It is the market maker's compensation and the impatient trader's fee, and it is the single most quoted measure of a market's quality. Careful usage distinguishes the *quoted* spread from the *effective* spread actually paid (which accounts for where inside the quotes you traded) and the *realised* spread (which accounts for how the price moved afterwards, and so measures what the liquidity provider kept).
