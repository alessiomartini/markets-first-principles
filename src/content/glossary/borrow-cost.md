---
term: "Borrow cost"
aliases: ["stock borrow fee", "short-selling cost", "borrowing fee"]
track: 7
status: written
---

The fee for borrowing a security in order to sell it short. It is small for most large, plentiful stocks and can reach many per cent a year for scarce ones, changing suddenly, and the lender can recall the shares and force the short seller to close. It falls hardest on the stocks where a short signal looks strongest, since those are the ones many others also want to short.
