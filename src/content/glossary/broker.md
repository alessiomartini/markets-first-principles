An intermediary who executes trades as your agent, for a commission, without taking the position onto its own book. The broker's obligation is to get you the best available execution rather than to profit from the other side of your trade — a distinction from a **dealer** that matters enormously and is blurred in practice, because most large institutions are both, and the same order can be handled either way depending on the desk it lands on.
