term: "Buyback"
aliases: ["share repurchase", "repurchase"]
track: 1
status: written
---

A company buying its own **share**s in the market and cancelling them. Economically it is close to a **dividend** — cash leaves the firm and goes to shareholders — but it is delivered by raising each remaining share's claim rather than by paying cash to everyone, which is treated differently by tax systems and reported differently in accounts. Buybacks are why earnings per share can rise while total earnings do not.
