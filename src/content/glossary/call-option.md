term: "Call option"
aliases: ["call", "calls"]
track: 1
status: written
---

The right, without the obligation, to buy the **underlying** at the **strike** on or before **expiry**. The asymmetry is the whole point: the holder participates in gains above the strike and simply walks away below it, so the payoff is bent rather than straight. That bend is what makes an option's value depend on **volatility** while a forward's does not.
