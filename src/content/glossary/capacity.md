The amount of capital a strategy can absorb before the cost of trading consumes its return. With impact costs that grow with the square root of size, the net return behaves like aK - cK^(3/2), so capacity depends on the square of the ratio of gross return to cost. Fast, high-turnover signals are often the best per unit of capital and the least able to absorb it.
