---
term: "Carry trade"
aliases: ["currency carry"]
track: 5
status: written
---

Borrowing in a low-interest currency to lend in a high-interest one, keeping the difference. Under **uncovered interest parity** it would earn zero on average; historically it has earned a positive return in small regular amounts, punctuated by sharp losses when funding currencies appreciate in risk-off episodes, so the premium is usually read as payment for crash risk.
