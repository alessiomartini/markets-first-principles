A central counterparty: an institution that steps between the two sides of a trade after matching, becoming buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer, so neither side is exposed to the other's default. This substitution — *novation* — is what makes anonymous exchange trading possible and is the reason you never learn who took the other side of your order. It does not abolish counterparty risk but concentrates it: the CCP itself becomes a systemically critical node, which is why it demands margin from everyone and why its own default waterfall is studied so obsessively.
