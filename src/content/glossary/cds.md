A contract paying out if a named borrower suffers a **default**, in exchange for a periodic fee — insurance on credit, tradable and available to people who do not own the underlying bond. That last property is what makes CDS both a useful hedging tool and a way to take large directional positions on someone else's failure, which is why they were central to the 2008 narrative.
