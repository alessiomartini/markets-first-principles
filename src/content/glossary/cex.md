A centralised crypto exchange: a company running a **matching engine** and an **order book**, holding customer funds, and settling trades on its internal ledger rather than on-chain. Functionally it is a traditional exchange, broker, clearing house and **custody** provider merged into one entity — a combination that securities regulation deliberately forbids, because each of those roles is a check on the others. The recurring failures of the sector have almost all been failures of that combination rather than of the underlying technology.
