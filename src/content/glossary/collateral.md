---
term: "Collateral"
aliases: ["pledged collateral", "eligible collateral"]
track: 6
status: written
---

An asset pledged to secure a loan or a derivative position, which the lender may keep or sell if the borrower defaults. Government bonds and cash are the most widely accepted. Lenders care about its quality, its liquidity and its price volatility, and they allow for these through haircuts and margin. When a particular security is scarce as collateral it trades special, borrowing it costs more, and failures to deliver it become more likely.
