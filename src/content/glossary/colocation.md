---

Renting rack space in the same building as the exchange's **matching engine**, so that the cable between your server and the market is metres rather than kilometres. Exchanges sell it openly and equalise cable lengths within the facility, which makes it a fair-but-expensive tier rather than a secret advantage. It exists because at these speeds the limiting factor is the speed of light in glass, and there is no way to buy that back once lost.
