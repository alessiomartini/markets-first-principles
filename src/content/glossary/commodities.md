Physical raw materials traded in **standardised** contracts: energy, metals, agricultural products. They differ from financial assets in the way that matters most for pricing — they must be stored, transported and eventually consumed, so their forward curves encode **storage** costs, transport bottlenecks and seasonal demand rather than pure discounting. A commodity is also the clearest case where the same name denotes different assets: Brent and WTI crude are both "oil" and trade at different prices for reasons of geography and grade.
