---
term: "Constant product formula"
aliases: ["x*y=k", "constant product market maker"]

---

The pricing rule of the simplest automated market maker: the product of the quantities of the two assets in the pool stays constant, xy = k, and the price is the ratio y/x. Buying one asset makes it dearer along a hyperbola, so a trade that is large relative to the pool pays a much worse price (slippage), and the size of the pool is the depth of the market.
