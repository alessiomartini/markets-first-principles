A futures curve where longer-dated contracts trade above nearer ones. For a storable asset this is the normal state, because it simply reflects the **cost of carry**. It is not a prediction that prices will rise — a point that survives being stated many times only because the mistake is so persistent — and it imposes a steady drag on anyone holding a long position through the **roll**.
