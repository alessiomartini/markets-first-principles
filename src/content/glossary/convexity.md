---

The curvature of the price-yield relationship: the second-order correction to **duration**. Positive convexity means the price rises more when yields fall than it falls when yields rise by the same amount, which is a genuine benefit and is priced accordingly. Convexity is why a duration hedge that is exact for small moves leaks money in large ones, and why it must be rebalanced.
