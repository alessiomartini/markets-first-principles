---
term: "Corporate action"
aliases: ["corporate actions"]
track: 0
status: written
---

Any event by which the issuer changes the terms of its own securities: dividends, splits, rights issues, buybacks, mergers, spin-offs. Corporate actions are where naive price series go wrong, because a two-for-one split halves the price without anyone losing money and a dividend drops the price by roughly the amount paid. Any analysis of returns must use a price series adjusted for them, and "the backtest worked until I used unadjusted prices" is one of the most common self-inflicted errors in the business.
