---
term: "Corridor system"
aliases: ["interest rate corridor"]
track: 5
status: written
---

A way of holding the overnight market rate close to the **policy rate** by setting two bounds. The central bank lends reserves to banks at a ceiling rate and pays interest on their deposits at a floor rate, so no bank will borrow from another above the ceiling or lend to another below the floor, and the market rate stays inside the corridor. The target usually sits near the middle. The system works when reserves are scarce enough that banks actually trade them with one another. Compare **floor system**.
