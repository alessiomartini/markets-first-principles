---
term: "Cost of carry"
aliases: ["carry", "carry cost"]
track: 1
status: written
---

The net cost of holding an asset from now until a future date: financing, **storage**, and insurance, less any income it produces. It is the quantity that links the **spot** and **forward price** through an arbitrage anyone with a warehouse and a loan can execute. When carry is negative — the asset pays more than it costs to hold — the forward price sits below spot, which is **backwardation**.
