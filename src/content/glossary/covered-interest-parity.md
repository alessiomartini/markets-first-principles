---
term: "Covered interest parity"
aliases: ["CIP"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The no-arbitrage relation that the forward premium on a currency equals the interest differential, so that borrowing in one currency and lending in another with the exchange rate hedged forward earns nothing. It held closely for major currencies until 2008; since then a persistent deviation, the cross-currency basis, has appeared, reflecting the cost of balance sheet and demand for dollars.
