The consumer price index, which tracks the cost of a fixed basket of goods and services bought by a typical household. It is the headline measure of inflation in most countries and the figure to which wages, pensions, rents and inflation-linked bonds are often indexed. The basket and its weights are updated periodically, and the treatment of housing, quality change and substitution between goods affects the result. A *core* measure excludes food and energy, whose prices are volatile, to show the underlying trend. Compare the **PCE deflator** and the **HICP**.
