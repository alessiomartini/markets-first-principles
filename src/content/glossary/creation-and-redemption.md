term: "Creation and redemption"

The mechanism that links an ETF's price to its contents. Authorised participants, large dealers, deliver a basket of securities to the fund and receive new shares (creation), or hand in shares and receive the basket (redemption), usually in blocks called creation units. When the ETF trades above its basket they create and sell, when below they buy and redeem, earning the gap less costs. It is an arbitrage, and it weakens when trading the underlying assets becomes costly or dealers lack balance sheet.
