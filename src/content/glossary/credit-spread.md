---
term: "Credit spread"
aliases: ["spread over treasuries", "credit risk premium"]
track: 1
status: written
---

The extra yield a bond offers over a government bond of the same maturity, compensating for the possibility of **default** and for the fact that the bond is harder to sell. Spreads widen in stress and narrow in calm, and they do so far more violently than default probabilities move, which means most of a spread's variation is a risk and liquidity premium rather than a changing forecast of failure.
