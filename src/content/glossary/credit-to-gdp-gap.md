---
term: "Credit-to-GDP gap"
aliases: ["credit gap"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The distance between private-sector credit as a share of GDP and its own long-run trend. Credit growing much faster than income cannot continue indefinitely, so a large positive gap is read as a warning of fragility; regulators use it to set the countercyclical capital buffer. Its weakness is that the trend has to be estimated using the very data that include the boom.
