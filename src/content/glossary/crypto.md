Assets whose ownership is recorded on a distributed ledger rather than by a custodian bank or a central securities depository. As a financial object a token is unremarkable — it is a claim with no cash flows, priced entirely on what someone else will pay — but the *plumbing* is genuinely different, which is what makes it worth a track of its own. Settlement is on-chain and near-instant, trading runs 24 hours a day with no open or close, and custody is a private key rather than a legal relationship, so the failure modes are technical rather than contractual.
