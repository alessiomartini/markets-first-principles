The business of holding assets on someone else's behalf and keeping the record of who owns what. In securities markets a custodian bank holds the position, collects the dividends, and its records are the legal proof of ownership, which means an investor's real exposure includes the custodian's soundness. In crypto the function is either replaced by a private key you hold yourself — no counterparty, no recourse if you lose it — or handed to an exchange that holds it for you, which reintroduces exactly the counterparty risk the design was supposed to remove, without the regulation.
