A venue that matches orders without displaying them beforehand, publishing the trade only after it happens. The purpose is legitimate and specific: an institution moving a large block in a lit **order book** reveals its intention and pays for it in **market impact**, so hiding the order until it executes is a way of protecting the trade rather than of hiding from regulators. The systemic objection is equally real — every order that migrates into the dark is one that no longer contributes to public price formation.
