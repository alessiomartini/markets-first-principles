The rule for converting a period between two dates into a fraction of a year: ACT/360, ACT/365, 30/360 and several others. It is pure convention with no economic content, and it differs by market and instrument, which means the same nominal rate produces different cash flows depending on which convention the contract names. It is also a reliable source of small, embarrassing discrepancies between two correct-looking implementations.
