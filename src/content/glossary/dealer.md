A firm that trades on its own account with customers, quoting a **bid** and an **ask** and holding the resulting inventory. Unlike a **broker**, who arranges someone else's trade for a commission, a dealer becomes your counterparty and takes the position onto its own balance sheet — so a dealer's willingness to quote depends on its capacity to warehouse risk, which is exactly what shrinks in a crisis. The two roles are often performed by the same institution, which is why the distinction is worth holding onto.
