term: "Default"
aliases: ["credit event", "defaulted"]

status: written
---

Failure to make a contractual payment, or breach of another term that triggers the same consequences. Default is not the same as bankruptcy and not the same as total loss: what matters to a bondholder is the *recovery rate*, the fraction eventually repaid, which for senior secured debt is often substantial. Pricing credit means estimating both the probability of the event and what is left afterwards.
