---
term: "Deleveraging"
aliases: ["deleverage"]
track: 5
status: written
---

Reducing debt relative to income or assets, by repayment, default or forced sale. Sensible for each borrower, but when many do it together it lowers spending and asset prices and so raises the debt burden of everyone else; the **leverage** that built up in a boom is unwound at the cost of output.
