---
term: "Delivery versus payment"
aliases: ["DvP", "payment versus payment"]
track: 6
status: written
---

A settlement rule under which the asset is delivered if and only if the payment is made, so that neither party can be left having paid without receiving, or delivered without being paid. It removes the principal risk in settlement. The corresponding rule for two currencies is payment versus payment, in which each currency leg is paid only if the other is; the failure of Bankhaus Herstatt in 1974 is the standard example of what happens without it.
