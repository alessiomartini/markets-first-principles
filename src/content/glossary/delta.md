The sensitivity of an option's price to a small change in the price of the **underlying**, $\Delta = \partial V / \partial S$. A call has a delta between 0 and 1 and a put between -1 and 0, so a delta of 0.4 means the option gains roughly 0.4 for each 1 the underlying rises. Delta is also the quantity of the underlying that offsets the option locally, which is why it doubles as the **hedge ratio** for the option. It is a local slope, not a constant: it changes as the underlying moves, as time passes and as volatility changes.
