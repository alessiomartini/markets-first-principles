The quantity resting in the **order book** at each price level, or cumulatively out to some distance from the mid. Depth answers the question the **bid-ask spread** cannot: how much can I trade before the price moves against me? A tight spread with no depth behind it is a market that looks cheap and is not — an appearance that becomes common exactly when conditions deteriorate.
