A decentralised exchange: trading executed by a smart contract on-chain, with no operator holding your funds. Most do not use an **order book** at all, but an *automated market maker* — a formula that quotes a price as a function of the pool's inventory, so liquidity is supplied by depositors rather than by quoting firms. The trade-offs are stark and instructive: settlement is atomic and custody-free, while price discovery is slower, costs are paid in gas and slippage, and every pending transaction is visible to anyone who wants to trade ahead of it.
