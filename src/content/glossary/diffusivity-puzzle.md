---
term: "Diffusivity puzzle"
aliases: ["diffusivity", "order flow puzzle"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The tension between two well-established facts: **order flow** is strongly and persistently autocorrelated, while prices are very nearly a martingale. If each trade moved the price by a fixed amount, persistent flow would make prices trend and the market trivially predictable. The resolution is the **propagator** — impact decays with a power law whose exponent is tied to the persistence of flow, so the predictable component cancels almost exactly. It is the tightest quantitative link in the field between microstructure and price statistics.
