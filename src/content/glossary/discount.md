term: "Discount"
aliases: ["discount to NAV", "discount to net asset value"]

The amount by which a fund share's market price is below its net asset value, as a fraction of NAV; the opposite is a premium. For ETFs it is normally tiny and short-lived because of creation and redemption, and it widens when the underlying assets are hard to trade, as in bond ETFs in March 2020. For closed-end funds, which have no such mechanism, it can persist for years.
