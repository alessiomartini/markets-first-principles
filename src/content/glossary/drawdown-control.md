---
term: "Drawdown control"
aliases: ["drawdown limit", "stop-loss", "risk limits"]
track: 7
status: written
---

Rules that limit how far a portfolio can fall from its peak: limits on size and concentration, cuts in position size when volatility rises, and reductions in exposure when a loss threshold is hit. They protect capital and keep investors from redeeming at the worst moment, but cutting after a loss locks the loss in and misses the recovery of a mean-reverting strategy, so the rule must be tested as part of the strategy.
