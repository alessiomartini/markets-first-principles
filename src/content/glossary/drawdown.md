The decline from a portfolio's running peak to its subsequent trough, usually quoted as a percentage, with *maximum drawdown* being the worst such decline over a period. It is the risk measure practitioners actually feel, because it is a statement about the path rather than the endpoint, and because investors redeem on drawdowns rather than on variance. Drawdown is sensitive to exactly the properties that **volatility** hides: autocorrelation in the tails, clustering of bad days, and the fact that large losses arrive together.
