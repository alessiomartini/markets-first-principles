The sensitivity of a bond's price to a change in its **yield to maturity**, and equivalently the weighted average time at which its cash flows arrive. A duration of seven means a one percent rise in yields costs roughly seven percent of the price. It is the standard unit in which interest rate risk is measured and hedged, and it is a first-order approximation whose error grows with the size of the move.
