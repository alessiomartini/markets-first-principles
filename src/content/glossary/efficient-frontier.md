---
term: "Efficient frontier"
aliases: ["mean-variance frontier"]
track: 4
status: written
physics: "The Pareto front of a two-objective optimisation, here risk against expected return."
---

The set of portfolios for which no other portfolio offers more expected return at the same variance or less variance at the same expected return, drawn as a curve in the plane of volatility and expected return. It is computed from estimated inputs, so a frontier measured in one sample is optimistic about the next: portfolios on it deliver less return for their risk out of sample.
