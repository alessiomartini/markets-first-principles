The claim that prices already reflect available information, so that no strategy using that information earns excess returns. It comes in three strengths — weak (past prices), semi-strong (public information), strong (all information, including private) — and its useful form is none of them, because as stated it is untestable without also specifying a model of what returns *should* be. The practitioner version is sharper and empirically live: which predictabilities survive transaction costs, at what horizon, and at what capacity.
