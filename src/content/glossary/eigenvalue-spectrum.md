The set of eigenvalues of a **correlation matrix**, and the diagnostic that reveals how much of it is noise. For pure noise the spectrum has a known shape — the Marchenko–Pastur distribution — depending only on the ratio $q = N/T$. Empirical spectra match it closely over most of their range, with a handful of eigenvalues escaping above the upper edge: one very large one corresponding to the market mode, and a few smaller ones corresponding to sectors. Everything inside the bulk is indistinguishable from noise.
