**Market capitalisation** plus net debt: what it would cost to buy the whole business free of its financing. It is the right measure when comparing companies with different capital structures, because equity value alone depends on how much of the firm is funded by borrowing. A company can have a small equity value and an enormous enterprise value, which is precisely the situation in which equity behaves like a call option on the assets.
