term: "Equity"

A residual claim on a company: after employees, suppliers, lenders and the tax authority have been paid, whatever remains belongs to the shareholders. That position at the back of the queue is the whole character of the asset — it is why equity absorbs the first losses, why it has no ceiling on the upside, and why its value can go to zero while the company continues to exist under its creditors. Everything else about equity, from voting rights to dividends to why it is more volatile than the underlying business, follows from being the residual.
