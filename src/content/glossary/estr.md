The euro short-term rate, published by the European Central Bank since October 2019 and based on overnight unsecured borrowing by banks in the euro area. It replaced the EONIA benchmark, which was discontinued in January 2022, and is the euro counterpart of SOFR and of the sterling SONIA. Unlike SOFR it is unsecured, so it includes a small credit component.
