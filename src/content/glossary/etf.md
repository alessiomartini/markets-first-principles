An exchange-traded fund: a fund whose shares trade on an exchange throughout the day like a stock, while the fund holds a basket of assets, usually following an index. Investors trade with each other at a market price, which can differ from the fund's net asset value; authorised participants keep the gap small by creating and redeeming shares against the basket. The first US ETF, tracking the S&P 500, began trading in 1993.
