---
term: "Excess volatility"
aliases: ["excess volatility puzzle", "Shiller puzzle"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The finding that stock prices move far more than any plausible measure of the fundamentals they are supposed to discount. Shiller's version compares the observed volatility of prices with the volatility of the perfect-foresight present value of subsequent dividends, and finds the former is many times larger. It is among the most durable challenges to a purely informational account of prices, and it points toward the mechanical account in Track 2: prices move because orders arrive.
