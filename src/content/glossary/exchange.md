A venue that centralises trading interest under published rules: it accepts orders from members, matches them by an announced algorithm, and publishes the results. The defining features are the rulebook and the publicity, not the technology. An exchange is *order-driven*: prices come from the interaction of participants' orders in the **order book**, rather than from a dealer quoting a two-sided price on request, which is how a *quote-driven* or over-the-counter market works.
