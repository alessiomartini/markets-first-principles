When an option may be exercised: *European* only at **expiry**, *American* at any time before it, *Bermudan* on specified dates. The distinction is not cosmetic — an American option is worth at least as much as its European twin, and valuing it requires solving an optimal stopping problem rather than evaluating a formula, which is why it usually needs a numerical method.
