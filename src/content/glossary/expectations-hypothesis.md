---
term: "Expectations hypothesis"
aliases: ["expectations theory", "pure expectations hypothesis"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The proposition that a long-dated yield is the average of the short rates expected over its life, so that each **forward rate** is the market's expected future short rate and the shape of the **yield curve** is a pure forecast. The pure form is rejected empirically: forward rates also predict excess bond returns, which means long yields carry a time-varying **term premium**. It survives as a useful decomposition, with the premium as the residual, and as a benchmark against which departures are measured.
