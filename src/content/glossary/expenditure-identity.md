---
term: "Expenditure identity"
aliases: ["GDP identity", "national income identity"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The accounting statement that GDP equals total spending on domestic output: $Y = C + I + G + (X - M)$, with consumption, investment, government purchases and net exports. It is an identity, true by construction, and says nothing by itself about what causes what. Its use is to decompose a change in output into the spending categories that drove it, and to expose the accounting links between sectors: a country running a trade deficit must be financing it with the saving of someone, in some form, which is where the connection to capital flows and exchange rates starts.
