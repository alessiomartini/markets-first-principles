term: "Expiry"
aliases: ["expiration", "maturity", "tenor"]

---

The date on which an option ceases to exist. Time is an asset to an option holder, since more time means more chance of a favourable move, so an option's value decays as expiry approaches — the decay measured by *theta*. The decay is not linear: it accelerates sharply in the final weeks for options near the money.
