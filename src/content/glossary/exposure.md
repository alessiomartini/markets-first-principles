---
term: "Exposure"
aliases: ["economic exposure", "risk exposure"]
track: 1
status: written
---

How much money a position gains or loses per unit move in something. Exposure is the economically meaningful quantity, and it is what **notional** is routinely mistaken for: a large notional with offsetting legs can carry almost no exposure, and a small notional in a highly convex instrument can carry a great deal.
