An input to a forecast: a quantity computed from data available at the time of the decision, such as the past month's return, a valuation ratio, a revision to an analyst's estimate or the imbalance in the order book. Features are raw material, and most have no predictive power by themselves; the more of them are tried on the same history, the more likely it is that one looks useful by chance.
