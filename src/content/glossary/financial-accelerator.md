The feedback by which a borrower's net worth affects its cost of borrowing. Lenders cannot observe borrowers fully, so firms with less net worth pay a higher premium over the safe rate; a downturn lowers net worth, raises the **credit spread** faced by borrowers, cuts investment and deepens the downturn. Formalised by Bernanke, Gertler and Gilchrist (1999).
