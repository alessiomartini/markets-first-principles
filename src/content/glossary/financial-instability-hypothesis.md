---
term: "Financial instability hypothesis"
aliases: ["Minsky hypothesis", "Minsky moment"]
track: 5
status: written
---

Hyman Minsky's claim that a stable economy breeds its own instability: after a calm period borrowers drift from financing where income covers interest and principal, to financing that covers only interest, to financing that depends on borrowing more, until lenders stop rolling over debt. It is a hypothesis with plausible examples and some support, not a settled model with a predictable timing.
