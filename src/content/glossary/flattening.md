---
term: "Flattening"
aliases: ["curve flattening", "flattener"]
track: 5
status: written
---

A narrowing of the gap between long-dated and short-dated yields. A *bear* flattening comes from short yields rising faster than long ones, typically as the central bank tightens; a *bull* flattening from long yields falling faster, as when investors expect weaker growth. A curve that keeps flattening can end in **curve inversion**. Contrast *steepening*, and note that the label describes the change in the spread, not what is happening to the level of rates.
