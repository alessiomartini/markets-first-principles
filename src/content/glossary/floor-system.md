---
term: "Floor system"
aliases: ["ample reserves regime"]
track: 5
status: written
---

A way of controlling the overnight market rate when reserves are abundant, typically after large asset purchases (**quantitative easing**). The central bank pays interest on reserves, and because no bank will lend reserves to the market for less than it earns at the central bank, that rate becomes the effective floor and in practice the policy rate itself. The central bank no longer needs to adjust the quantity of reserves day to day, but its balance sheet stays large. Compare **corridor system**.
