A quantity measured per unit of time: income per year, output per quarter, spending per month. Its dimension includes time, and it only means something over a stated period. GDP is a flow, as are saving, investment and the trade balance. A flow accumulates into a **stock**: wealth at a date is the sum of past saving, and a debt grows by the deficits run on it. Confusing the two is the commonest mistake in reading economic figures, for instance comparing a debt, which is a stock, with a deficit, which is a flow.
