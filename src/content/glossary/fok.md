Fill-or-kill: execute the order in full immediately, or cancel it entirely. Unlike an **IOC** it forbids partial execution, which makes it the right instruction when a half-done trade is worse than none — one leg of a two-leg arbitrage, for instance. It is rarer than IOC precisely because partial fills are usually acceptable and full availability at one instant usually is not.
