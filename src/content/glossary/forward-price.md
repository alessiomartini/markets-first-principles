The price agreed today for a transaction that settles at a fixed future date. For a storable asset it is pinned by arbitrage to the **spot** price plus the **cost of carry**, which is why it is emphatically not a forecast — a point worth repeating because the contrary belief is the most common misreading in commodities. A forward is bilateral and customised; its exchange-traded, **standardisation** cousin is a future.
