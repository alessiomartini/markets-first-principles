---
term: "Forward rate"
aliases: ["forward rates", "implied forward"]
track: 1
status: written

The interest rate for a future period, implied today by the **zero-coupon curve** rather than quoted directly: if you know what borrowing for one year costs and what borrowing for two costs, the rate for the second year alone follows by arbitrage. Forward rates are the local structure of the curve, where **yield to maturity** is its integrated form, which is why a smooth-looking yield curve can imply wildly oscillating forwards if it was interpolated carelessly. They are frequently misread as the market's forecast of future rates; they are that forecast plus a term premium, and the two cannot be separated by looking at the curve alone.
