---
term: "Fractional Kelly"
aliases: ["half Kelly", "fractional Kelly bet"]
track: 4
status: written
---

Betting a fixed fraction of the **Kelly criterion** amount, most often one half. Growth is flat near its maximum, so half of the Kelly size keeps about three quarters of the maximum long-run growth rate while cutting the volatility of wealth and the depth of **drawdowns** roughly in half. The main reason to do it is that the edge is estimated, not known: an estimate chosen because history looked good tends to be too high, and sizing to it overbets, which costs much more than underbetting by the same amount.
