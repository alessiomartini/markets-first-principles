---
term: "Free float"
aliases: ["float", "public float"]
track: 0
status: written
---

The portion of a company's shares actually available to trade, excluding stakes held by founders, governments, cross-holdings and other strategic owners that do not turn over. It matters twice: it determines how much can genuinely be bought without moving the price, and it is the weighting most major indices use, so a change in free float forces mechanical buying or selling from every fund tracking the index. A company with a large market capitalisation and a small float is a thin market wearing a large number.
