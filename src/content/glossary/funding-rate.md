---
term: "Funding rate"
aliases: ["funding payment", "funding"]

status: written
---

The periodic payment between longs and shorts of a perpetual future, commonly every eight hours. When the perpetual trades above the index the rate is positive and longs pay shorts; when below, shorts pay longs. It makes the premium costly to hold and pays arbitrageurs to close it. A baseline of 0.01 per cent per eight hours is about 11 per cent a year. It is paid between traders, not to the exchange.
