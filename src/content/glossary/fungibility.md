Whether any one unit is an acceptable substitute for any other. One share of a company is interchangeable with any other share of the same class; one barrel of Brent meeting the specification is interchangeable with any other; a specific house is interchangeable with nothing. Fungibility is what makes a liquid market possible at all, because it means a buyer can be matched with any seller rather than with the particular seller holding the particular item. Where it fails — property, art, private companies, bespoke swaps — you get search, negotiation and wide spreads instead of a continuous price.
