The market in which one currency is exchanged for another, and by daily turnover the largest market in the world. It has no central exchange: it is a network of bilateral dealing relationships between banks, and prices are quoted as a rate between an ordered pair, so every quote is simultaneously a price of one thing and the inverse price of another. That symmetry is why FX has no natural notion of "going up" — a rising EUR/USD is a falling USD/EUR, and any claim about the level is a claim about a pair.
