---
term: "GDP deflator"
aliases: ["implicit price deflator"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The ratio of **nominal GDP** to **real GDP**, expressed as an index: a price index for everything the economy produces. Unlike the **CPI** it is not based on a fixed basket; its weights follow what is actually produced each period, and it covers investment goods, government output and exports as well as consumer goods, while excluding imports. It is therefore a broad measure of domestic price pressure but a poor guide to the cost of living, and it is published only quarterly and revised.
