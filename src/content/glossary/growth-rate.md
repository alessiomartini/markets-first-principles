---
term: "Growth rate"
aliases: ["geometric growth rate", "log growth rate"]
track: 4
status: written
---

The average per-period rate at which capital compounds over a long run, measured as the mean of the logarithm of the wealth ratio from one period to the next. It differs from the average return: a gain of 50% followed by a loss of 50% has an average return of zero and leaves 75% of the starting capital, a growth rate of about $-14\%$ per period. For small returns the growth rate is approximately the mean return minus half the variance, so volatility directly reduces compounding. It is the quantity the **Kelly criterion** maximises.
