---
term: "Haircut"
aliases: ["collateral haircut", "margin haircut"]
track: 6
status: written
---

The percentage by which the value of collateral exceeds the loan it secures. A bond worth 100 pledged with a 2 per cent haircut borrows 98. It is the margin of the repo and securities-lending markets, it rises with the riskiness and illiquidity of the collateral, and the leverage a borrower can obtain is its inverse. Lenders can raise it quickly in stress, which acts like a margin call on every borrower at once.
