---
term: "Hedge ratio"
aliases: ["hedging ratio", "beta hedge"]
track: 1
status: written
---

How much of the hedging instrument to hold per unit of the exposure being hedged. It is an estimated quantity, usually a regression coefficient or a derivative, and it is unstable: the relationship that justified it can shift precisely when the hedge is needed. Getting the ratio wrong is a more common source of loss than choosing the wrong instrument.
