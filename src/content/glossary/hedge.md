---
term: "Hedge"
aliases: ["hedging", "hedged"]
track: 1
status: written
---

A position taken to offset an existing risk rather than to profit on its own. The defining feature is what you already own: the same trade is a hedge for someone with the opposite natural exposure and a speculation for someone without one. Hedging does not eliminate risk, it exchanges a large risk for a smaller **basis risk** — and it costs money, which is why the decision is never automatic.
