The harmonised index of consumer prices, the euro area's measure of inflation. It is computed to a common methodology across member states so that national rates are comparable, and it is the series that the European Central Bank targets. Its definition of the household basket differs from national indices, most visibly in how owner-occupied housing is treated, which it largely leaves out. Compare the **CPI** in the US and the **PCE deflator**.
