aliases: ["HFT"]

---

Trading strategies whose edge depends on speed, operating on horizons from microseconds to minutes and holding essentially no overnight risk. HFT is not one strategy but at least two with opposite social valences: electronic **market maker**s who supply liquidity and narrow spreads, and latency arbitrageurs who win **race condition**s against stale quotes. Most public argument about HFT founders on conflating them.
