---
term: "Impermanent loss"
aliases: ["divergence loss"]
track: 6
status: written
---

The shortfall of a liquidity provider's position in a constant product pool relative to simply holding the two assets, when the price moves. For a price ratio r it equals 2*sqrt(r)/(1+r) - 1, about 5.7 per cent for a doubling and 20 per cent for a fourfold change, before fees. It disappears if the price returns, hence the name, but is realised when the provider withdraws, and it is the counterpart of arbitrageurs' profit.
