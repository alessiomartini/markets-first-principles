---
term: "Information coefficient"
aliases: ["IC", "rank IC"]
track: 7
status: written
---

The correlation, across assets, between a signal on one date and the returns realised over the following horizon, averaged over dates. The rank version uses ranks and is less sensitive to extreme returns. Useful signals have small values, of a few hundredths; by Grinold's fundamental law the information ratio is about the IC times the square root of the number of independent bets.
