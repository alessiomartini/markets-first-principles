---
term: "Interest rate swap"
aliases: ["IRS", "swap"]
track: 1
status: written
---

An agreement to exchange a stream of fixed interest payments for a stream of floating ones on the same **notional**, which is never itself exchanged. It is the largest derivatives market in the world by notional and the main instrument through which institutions convert fixed exposure into floating or the reverse. A swap is economically a portfolio of forwards on the short rate, which is how it is priced.
