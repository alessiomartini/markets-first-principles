The tendency for activity to concentrate in bursts separated by quiet periods, rather than arriving at a steady rate. In returns it is the qualitative face of **volatility clustering**: most of the year's variance is delivered on a handful of days. The word is borrowed from turbulence, where it describes the same statistical structure, and the borrowing is unusually literal — the multifractal models used for financial volatility were first written down for velocity fields.
