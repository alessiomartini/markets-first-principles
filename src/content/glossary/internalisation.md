---
term: "Internalisation"
aliases: ["internaliser", "internalization"]
track: 0
status: written
---

A broker matching a customer's order against its own inventory, or against another customer's order, instead of routing it to an exchange. It is how most retail equity orders in the United States are executed. The customer typically gets a price slightly better than the public quote, which is a genuine benefit; the objection is that the flow being internalised is the uninformed flow that made public quoting profitable, so what reaches the exchange is systematically more toxic and the public spread widens for everyone.
