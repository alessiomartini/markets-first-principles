---
term: "Intrinsic value"
aliases: ["intrinsic"]
track: 1
status: written
---

What an option would be worth if it expired right now: the better of zero and the immediate exercise value. It is a floor on the **premium**, enforced by arbitrage, and it is the part of an option's price that behaves exactly like the **underlying**. Everything interesting about options lives in the other part.
