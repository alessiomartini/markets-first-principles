term: "Inventory risk"
aliases: ["inventory", "position risk"]

physics: "A random walk with a restoring force: the maker's position diffuses under order flow while it is continually pulled back toward zero by skewed quotes."
---

The risk a **market maker** carries from holding the positions its **quoting** leaves it with. A maker that buys from three sellers in a row is long whether it wanted to be or not, and must either widen or skew its quotes to attract the other side. Inventory risk is why a market maker's quotes are not symmetric around the fair value: they lean in whichever direction unloads the current position.
