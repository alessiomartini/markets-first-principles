---
term: "Kurtosis"
aliases: ["excess kurtosis", "leptokurtic"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The normalised fourth moment of a distribution, $\langle r^4 \rangle / \langle r^2 \rangle^2$; *excess* kurtosis subtracts the Gaussian value of 3, so that zero means "as fat-tailed as a Gaussian". Financial returns have large positive excess kurtosis at short horizons — often 10 or more for daily data — which is the crude first statement of the fat-tails story. Its weakness is that it is a single moment dominated by a handful of observations, and it does not exist at all if the tail exponent is 4 or less, which for many series it plausibly is.
