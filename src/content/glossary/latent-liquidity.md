The trading interest that exists but is not displayed — orders held in traders' heads, in algorithms waiting for a trigger, or in **iceberg order**s. The visible **order book** is a small fraction of the true supply and demand, which is why it is a poor predictor of what would actually trade at a given price. The latent order book models of modern microstructure take this seriously: they treat the displayed book as the exposed tip of a much larger density of intentions, and derive the square-root **market impact** law from its shape.
