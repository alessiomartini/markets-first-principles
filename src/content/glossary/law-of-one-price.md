---
term: "Law of one price"
aliases: ["LOP"]
track: 4
status: written
---

The principle that two assets or portfolios with identical payoffs in every state of the world must have the same price. If they did not, one could buy the cheaper and sell the dearer and lock in a profit with no risk, which is an **arbitrage**. The law is the basic step of **no-arbitrage** pricing: it lets the price of a complicated instrument be read off from a portfolio of simpler ones with the same payoffs. It is a statement about frictionless markets; in practice transaction costs, financing and limits on short selling mean prices can differ by a band rather than exactly.
