---
term: "Leverage"
aliases: ["leveraged", "gearing"]
track: 1
status: written
---

Controlling an exposure larger than the capital committed to it, whether through borrowing, **margin**, or the structure of a derivative. Leverage multiplies returns and losses symmetrically, but its danger is asymmetric: it introduces a level at which you are forced to sell regardless of your view. Almost every financial crisis is a story about leverage meeting a **margin** call.
