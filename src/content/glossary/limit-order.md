---
term: "Limit order"
aliases: ["limit orders", "passive order", "resting order"]
track: 2
status: written
---

An instruction to buy or sell up to a stated quantity, at a stated price or better, which rests in the **order book** until it is matched, cancelled, or expires. A limit order chooses price certainty and accepts execution uncertainty: it may never fill, and the times it does fill are correlated with the times you would rather it had not. It is called *passive* because it supplies liquidity to the book rather than removing it, and most venues charge it a lower fee — or pay it a rebate — for doing so.
