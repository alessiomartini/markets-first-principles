term: "Linear payoff"
aliases: ["linear instrument", "delta-one"]

---

A **payoff** that changes one-for-one with the **underlying**: forwards, futures, swaps, the asset itself. Linear instruments are easy in exactly one specific sense — their sensitivity does not change as the price moves, so a hedge put on today still works tomorrow. That is what "delta-one" means on a trading floor.
