Admission of a security to trading on an exchange, and the continuing obligations that come with it: publishing audited accounts, disclosing material information, meeting free-float and governance standards. A listing is therefore a bundle — access to a pool of buyers in exchange for submitting to disclosure — and it is the mechanism by which public equity markets generate the information everyone else's analysis depends on. Nothing equivalent gates a token's appearance on a crypto exchange, which is the single sharpest institutional difference between the two markets.
