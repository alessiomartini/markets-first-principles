---
term: "Long memory"
aliases: ["long-range dependence", "power-law memory"]
track: 3
status: written
---

Autocorrelation that decays as a power law rather than exponentially, so that dependence persists over horizons far longer than any characteristic time. Absolute returns show it clearly: their autocorrelation remains significant over months. It matters because a power law has no natural scale, so there is no window long enough to average it away, and estimators that assume independence beyond some lag remain biased however much data you collect.
