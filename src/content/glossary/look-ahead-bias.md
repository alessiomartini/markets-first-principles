---
term: "Look-ahead bias"
aliases: ["lookahead bias", "peeking", "point-in-time"]
track: 7
status: written
---

The use, at a date in a simulation, of information that was not yet available: accounts taken from the quarter they describe instead of the date they were published, figures as restated today instead of as first reported, or a trade at the same close that produced the signal. It makes a strategy appear to know the future. Data stored point-in-time, with the date each value became known, are the standard cure.
