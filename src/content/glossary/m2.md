A measure of the money supply that adds to currency and demand deposits the more liquid savings instruments: savings accounts, small time deposits and retail money market funds. The exact definition differs between countries and has been revised over time. It is a measure of the money the public holds, as opposed to the **monetary base**, which is what the central bank issues. The link between its growth and inflation is strong over long horizons and unreliable over short ones, which is why most central banks stopped targeting it.
