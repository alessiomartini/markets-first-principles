---
term: "Margin call"
aliases: ["margin calls", "variation margin call"]
track: 6
status: written
---

A demand from a broker or clearing house to post additional **margin** because losses on a position have reduced the account below the required level. It must usually be met quickly, in cash or acceptable collateral, and if it is not the position can be closed out by force. Margin calls turn **leverage** into a timing problem: a position can be right in the long run and still be liquidated because the loss arrived before the funding did. When many holders are called at once and sell to raise cash, the selling can push prices further and generate more calls.
