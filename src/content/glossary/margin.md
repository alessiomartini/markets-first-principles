Collateral posted to cover potential losses on a leveraged position. *Initial* margin is put up at the start and sized to cover a plausible adverse move; *variation* margin is exchanged daily as the position gains or loses value, which is the mechanism that keeps a futures contract's credit exposure near zero. Margin is also the transmission channel of most financial crises: falling prices trigger calls, calls force selling, selling lowers prices.
