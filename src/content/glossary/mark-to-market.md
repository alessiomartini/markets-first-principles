---
term: "Mark-to-market"
aliases: ["marking to market", "MTM"]
track: 1
status: written
---

Revaluing a position at current market prices, and — for futures — actually paying or receiving the change in value each day. Daily settlement is what makes a futures contract structurally different from a **forward price** agreement of the same terms: the exposure is reset to zero every day, so nobody accumulates a large claim on anybody. It also means a futures position generates cash flows before it expires, which a forward does not.
