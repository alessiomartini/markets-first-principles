Behaviour that damages confidence in the fairness of a market: trading on non-public price-sensitive information, passing such information on, and manipulation, which includes false information, wash trades and spoofing (placing orders with the intent to cancel them to mislead others). The European Market Abuse Regulation of 2016 sets out these offences; in the US the general rule is Section 10(b) and Rule 10b-5, and spoofing is a specific offence since Dodd-Frank.
