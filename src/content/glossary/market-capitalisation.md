Share price multiplied by shares outstanding. It is the standard measure of a company's equity size and it is routinely over-read: it is a *marginal* price extrapolated to every share, so it is not the amount anyone could realise by selling, and it is certainly not money that has been invested in the company. For a name with small **free float**, the gap between market cap and realisable value is enormous.
