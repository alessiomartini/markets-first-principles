term: "Market maker"
aliases: ["market makers", "liquidity provider"]

A participant who continuously quotes both a **bid** and an **ask**, standing ready to buy from sellers and sell to buyers, and earning the **bid-ask spread** for the service. The business is not directional: the market maker wants to end the day flat, having captured the spread many times. What makes it hard is *adverse selection* — the people most eager to hit your quote are disproportionately the ones who know something you do not, so the spread has to be wide enough to cover the losses to informed traders out of the profits from uninformed ones.
