---
term: "Market order"
aliases: ["market orders", "aggressive order", "marketable order"]
track: 2
status: written
---

An instruction to trade a stated quantity immediately at whatever prices the **order book** currently offers. It chooses execution certainty and accepts price uncertainty: a large market order walks up through successive levels of the book, and the average price it achieves is worse than the quoted best price by an amount that grows with size. It is called *aggressive* because it removes liquidity, and it generally pays a higher fee for the privilege.
