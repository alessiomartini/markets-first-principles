The European Union's rulebook for investment firms, trading venues and markets in financial instruments, in force since 3 January 2018 and replacing the first MiFID of 2007. It extends transparency rules to bonds and derivatives, caps trading in dark pools, defines systematic internalisers, regulates algorithmic trading, strengthens best execution and separates payment for research from execution commissions.
