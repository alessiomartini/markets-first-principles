---
term: "Monetary base"
aliases: ["base money", "high-powered money", "M0"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The money created by the central bank: physical currency in circulation plus the reserves that commercial banks hold in accounts at the central bank. It is the ultimate settlement asset of the system. It is much smaller than the broad money that households and firms actually hold, because most of that is created by banks making loans and is a claim on a bank rather than on the central bank. Expanding the base, as during **quantitative easing**, does not by itself expand broad money by any fixed multiple; that depends on whether banks and borrowers want to lend and borrow. Compare **M2**.
