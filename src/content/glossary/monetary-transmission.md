---
term: "Monetary transmission"
aliases: ["transmission mechanism", "monetary policy transmission"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The chain by which a change in the **policy rate** reaches output and inflation. It runs through several channels at once: market interest rates and mortgage costs change, asset prices and the exchange rate move, banks alter how freely they lend, and expectations about future policy shift. Each takes time, and the lags are long and variable, usually put at one to two years for inflation. This is why central banks act on forecasts rather than on current data, and why the same rate change can have a different effect in different periods.
