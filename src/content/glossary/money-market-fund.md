A pooled fund that invests in short-term, high-quality debt such as Treasury bills, repo and commercial paper, and aims to keep a stable value per share. It lets households and firms hold something close to cash while providing a large part of the short-term lending that dealers and banks rely on. Because investors can withdraw at once, it is vulnerable to runs, as the Reserve Primary Fund showed in September 2008 and as prime funds did again in March 2020.
