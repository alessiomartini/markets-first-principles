---
term: "Moneyness"
aliases: ["in the money", "out of the money", "at the money", "ATM", "ITM", "OTM"]
track: 1
status: written
---

How far an option's **strike** sits from the current price, and therefore whether exercising would pay. *In the money* would pay now, *out of the money* would not, *at the money* sits at the current price. Moneyness is usually expressed in units of expected move rather than in currency, because an option 5% away means something completely different for a utility stock and for a crypto token.
