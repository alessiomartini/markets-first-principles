---
term: "Multifractality"
aliases: ["multifractal", "multiscaling"]
track: 3
status: written
physics: "The same formalism as the multifractal spectrum in turbulence: a continuum of scaling exponents rather than one, indicating that different moments scale differently."
---

The property that different moments of the return distribution scale with different exponents, so that $\mathbb{E}|r_\tau|^q \sim \tau^{\zeta(q)}$ with $\zeta$ nonlinear in $q$. A simple random walk gives $\zeta(q) = q/2$, a straight line; real returns give a curve. The nonlinearity is the signature that no single **Hurst exponent** describes the process, and it is the sharpest quantitative link between financial time series and turbulent cascades.
