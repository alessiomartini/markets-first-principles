term: "Multiple testing"
aliases: ["multiple comparisons", "data snooping bias", "look-elsewhere effect"]

---

The problem that a significance threshold controls the chance of a false discovery in one test only: with one hundred skill-free rules tested at the 5 per cent level, about five pass. Corrections such as Bonferroni's, White's reality check and false-discovery-rate procedures adjust for the number of tests; Harvey, Liu and Zhu (2016) argued for a t-statistic of about 3 for new factors. Particle physics knows it as the look-elsewhere effect.
