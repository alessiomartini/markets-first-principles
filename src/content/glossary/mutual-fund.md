term: "Mutual fund"
aliases: ["open-end fund", "mutual funds"]

An open-end fund that issues and redeems shares directly with investors at the net asset value calculated after each day's close, so there is no market price to differ from NAV. The weakness is that redemptions are paid out of the fund's assets, so the cost of selling illiquid holdings falls on the investors who remain, which gives investors in such funds a reason to leave early. Regulated in the US under the Investment Company Act of 1940 and in Europe mainly as UCITS.
