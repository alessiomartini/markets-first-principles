---
term: "NAIRU"
aliases: ["non-accelerating inflation rate of unemployment", "natural rate of unemployment"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The rate of unemployment at which inflation neither accelerates nor decelerates, given what people expect. Below it, inflation tends to rise; above it, to fall. The name comes from the policy question it answers: how low can unemployment be pushed before inflation starts to climb. It is not observed but inferred from the behaviour of inflation, its estimates carry wide error bands and have moved with demographics, institutions and the way labour markets work, and the closely related *natural rate* of Friedman and Phelps is a theoretical concept that the NAIRU is used to approximate.
