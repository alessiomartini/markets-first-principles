---
term: "Natural exposure"
aliases: ["natural position", "structural exposure"]
track: 1
status: written
---

Risk that arises from doing business rather than from choosing to take a position: an airline's need for fuel, a miner's inventory of ore, an exporter's foreign revenue. Natural exposure is what makes hedging a service rather than a bet, and it is the reason derivatives markets exist at all — someone with a natural short needs someone willing to be long.
