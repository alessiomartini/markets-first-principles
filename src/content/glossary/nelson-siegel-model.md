---
term: "Nelson-Siegel model"
aliases: ["Nelson-Siegel", "Nelson-Siegel-Svensson"]
track: 5
status: written
---

A parametric description of the **yield curve** with a small number of parameters that are read as a level, a slope and a curvature, plus a decay parameter that sets the maturity of the hump. Introduced by Nelson and Siegel in 1987 and extended by Svensson with a second hump, it is widely used by central banks and data providers to smooth and interpolate observed yields. It is a fitting device and not a theory: it fixes the shape, not the reason for the shape, and the **forward rate**s it implies depend on the fit.
