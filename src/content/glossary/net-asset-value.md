aliases: ["NAV"]

---

The market value of a fund's assets less its liabilities, divided by its number of shares. Mutual fund investors deal at the NAV calculated after each day's close; ETF prices are compared with it to measure premium or discount. For funds holding assets that trade rarely, such as many bonds, the NAV is built from estimated prices and can lag the true value when markets move fast.
