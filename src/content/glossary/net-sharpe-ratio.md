---
term: "Net Sharpe ratio"
aliases: ["net-of-cost Sharpe ratio", "after-cost Sharpe"]
track: 7
status: written
---

The Sharpe ratio of a strategy's returns after spread, impact, borrow, financing and other costs have been deducted: (gross return minus turnover times cost per unit traded) divided by volatility. It is the only version on which the decision to run a strategy should rest. A gross Sharpe ratio of 1.3 can become 1.0 at 20 times annual turnover and 10 basis points per trade, and a negative one at 100 times.
