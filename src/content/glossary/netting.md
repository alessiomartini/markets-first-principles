---
term: "Netting"
aliases: ["multilateral netting", "bilateral netting"]
track: 6
status: written
---

Replacing many gross obligations with a few net ones, so that only the difference has to move. Bilateral netting offsets what two parties owe each other; multilateral netting, which a CCP makes possible, offsets what every member owes the system. It cuts the amount of cash and securities that must be delivered and the exposure to a failure, and it relies on the legal enforceability of the offset when one party is insolvent.
