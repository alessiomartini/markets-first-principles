---
term: "Neutralisation"
aliases: ["neutralization", "market neutral", "factor neutral"]
track: 7
status: written
---

Removing a portfolio's exposure to chosen factors, such as the market, industries or styles, either by subtracting the factor-explained part of the signal before building the portfolio or by constraining the exposures to zero when choosing weights. What is left is meant to be the signal's own contribution. It costs some gross return and extra trading, and it is neutral only to the factors in the risk model.
