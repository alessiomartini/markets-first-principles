---
term: "No-arbitrage"
aliases: ["absence of arbitrage", "arbitrage-free"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The condition that no portfolio can be constructed which costs nothing, can never lose, and sometimes gains. It is far weaker than **efficient market hypothesis** and far more useful: it does not require anyone to be rational or informed, only that free money is taken quickly. Almost all of derivative pricing follows from no-arbitrage alone, which is why those results are robust in a way that predictions about expected returns are not.
