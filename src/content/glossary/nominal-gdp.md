term: "Nominal GDP"
aliases: ["nominal output"]

The value of all final goods and services produced in an economy over a period, measured at the prices of that period. It rises when more is produced and also when prices rise, so a growing figure alone does not say which. Nominal GDP is the natural scale for ratios such as debt to GDP, and it is what a central bank's policy ultimately has to be compared with when the question is how much money income there is to service nominal debts. Contrast **real GDP**.
