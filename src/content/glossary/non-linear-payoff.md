term: "Non-linear payoff"
aliases: ["convex payoff", "non-linear instrument"]
track: 1
status: written
---

A **payoff** whose sensitivity to the **underlying** itself changes as the underlying moves — options and anything built from them. Non-linearity is why option books must be re-hedged continuously, why **volatility** enters the price at all, and why the mathematics of Track 4 is harder than the mathematics of Track 1.
