The reference quantity on which a derivative's payments are computed — and, for a swap, an amount that never changes hands. Notional is not exposure and not value: a $100m interest rate swap might have a market value of a few hundred thousand and a risk equivalent to a much smaller bond position. Confusing the two is how the "$600 trillion derivatives market" headline is manufactured.
