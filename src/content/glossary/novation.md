The legal replacement of one contract by another. In a cleared market the contract between the original buyer and seller is cancelled the moment the trade is accepted by the clearing house and is replaced by two contracts, one between the CCP and each side. It is the step that makes the CCP the counterparty to everyone, and the reason a trader on an exchange never needs to know, or to assess, who is on the other side.
