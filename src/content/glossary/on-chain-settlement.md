---
term: "On-chain settlement"
aliases: ["onchain settlement", "settlement on-chain"]
track: 6
status: written
---

Settlement in which the transfer of the asset is recorded directly on the shared ledger and is the settlement itself, with no later update of legal ownership. Finality is probabilistic and takes time, about ten minutes per block on Bitcoin and roughly thirteen minutes to finality on Ethereum. A program can exchange two assets in one transaction so that both legs happen or neither does, which gives delivery versus payment without an intermediary.
