The complete set of **limit orders** currently resting at a venue, organised by price and, within each price, by time of arrival. The book is the market's memory of everyone's stated willingness to trade: the highest buy order is the **bid**, the lowest sell order is the **ask**, and the gap between them is the **bid-ask spread**. Traders say *the book* for this object and *the touch* for its best two levels. Prices do not exist independently and then get recorded in the book — the book is where a price is produced, when an incoming **market order** consumes resting volume.
