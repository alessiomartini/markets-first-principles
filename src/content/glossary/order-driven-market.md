---
term: "Order-driven market"
aliases: ["order driven", "auction market"]
track: 0
status: written
---

A market where the price emerges from participants' own orders meeting in a central **order book**, with no intermediary obliged to quote. Anyone may post a **limit order** and anyone may hit it, so the best price is whatever the crowd has posted. The strength is transparency and equal access; the weakness is that in a stressed or thin market there may simply be nothing on the other side, because nobody has an obligation to be there.
