---
term: "Order-flow imbalance"
aliases: ["OFI", "trade imbalance"]
track: 2
status: written
---

The net of buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated volume over an interval, and the single best short-horizon predictor of price change there is. Its explanatory power is high enough to be uncomfortable — over short windows imbalance explains most of the variance in returns — and it is not a trading strategy, because you would have to know the imbalance before it happened.
