term: "Order flow"
aliases: ["flow", "signed order flow"]

status: written
---

The stream of buy and sell orders arriving at a market, usually signed by which side initiated the trade. Order flow is the immediate cause of price changes — prices move because orders arrive, not because value changed — and it is strongly autocorrelated, persisting for hours or days, because large orders are split into many small ones. That persistence, combined with prices that remain nearly unpredictable, is the central puzzle Track 2 exists to explain.
