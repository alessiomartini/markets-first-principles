---
term: "Output gap"
aliases: ["GDP gap"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The difference between actual real GDP and *potential output*, the level the economy could sustain without pushing inflation up or down, usually expressed as a percentage of potential. A positive gap means the economy is running hot and a negative one that resources are idle. Potential output is not observed: it is estimated with filters or production-function models, and estimates are revised heavily as new data arrive, so the gap measured in real time is a poor guide to the gap that is later reported.
