---
term: "Over-the-counter"
aliases: ["OTC", "off-exchange"]
track: 0
status: written
---

Trading done bilaterally between two parties rather than through an exchange's central book. The terms are negotiated — often by request for quote to several dealers — and the trade may be reported publicly late or not at all. OTC is how most bonds, swaps, and FX trade, so by notional it is the larger part of finance by a wide margin. The trade-offs are consistent: OTC gives you customised contracts and discretion about revealing your intentions, and costs you price transparency and, historically, a live **counterparty** exposure.
