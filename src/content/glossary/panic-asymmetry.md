---
term: "Panic asymmetry"
aliases: ["leverage effect asymmetry", "down-vol asymmetry"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The empirical fact that volatility rises much more after price falls than after equivalent rises. The name "leverage effect" comes from an old explanation — a falling equity value raises a firm's debt-to-equity ratio and therefore its risk — but the effect is far too fast and too large to be explained that way, and appears in indices and assets with no leverage at all. Panic is the better description: selling begets selling in a way that buying does not beget buying.
