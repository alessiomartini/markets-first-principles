---
term: "Partial fill"
aliases: ["partially filled"]
track: 2
status: written
---

Execution of some but not all of an order's quantity, leaving the remainder either resting or cancelled depending on the instruction attached. Partial fills are the normal case rather than the exception, and they are informative: an order that fills instantly and completely usually means the price is about to move against you, while one that fills slowly is being adversely selected more gently.
