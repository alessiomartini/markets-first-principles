A wholesaler paying a retail broker to route its customers' orders to be **internalisation** rather than sent to an exchange. The economics are transparent once **adverse selection** is understood: **retail flow** is on average uninformed, so it is profitable to trade against, and the wholesaler shares some of that profit with the broker and some with the customer via price improvement. The systemic objection is equally clear — removing the uninformed flow raises the toxicity of what reaches the exchange, so public spreads widen for everyone.
