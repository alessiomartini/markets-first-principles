---
term: "Payoff"
aliases: ["payoff diagram", "terminal payoff"]
track: 1
status: written
---

What an instrument pays at **expiry** as a function of the **underlying**'s price then — a straight line for a forward, a bent line for an option. The payoff is the contract's definition, known with certainty; everything hard about pricing is working out what that certain future shape is worth now, under uncertainty about where the underlying will be.
