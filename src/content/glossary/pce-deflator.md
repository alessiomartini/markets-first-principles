The price index for personal consumption expenditures in the US national accounts, and the measure the Federal Reserve uses for its inflation target. It differs from the **CPI** in coverage, because it includes spending made on households' behalf such as employer-paid health care, and in method, because its weights adjust as consumers shift between goods, which usually makes it run somewhat lower. Its core version is the series that most closely tracks what the Fed says it is aiming for.
