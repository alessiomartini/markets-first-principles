---
term: "Perishability"
aliases: ["perishable", "non-storable"]
track: 0
status: written
---

The rate at which an asset stops being the thing that was traded. Fresh produce perishes in days, electricity perishes instantly, a newspaper's information perishes overnight; a share of stock does not perish at all. Perishability is the limiting case of costly **storage**, and it has a sharp consequence: for a perishable good, each delivery period is effectively a separate market with its own supply and demand, so prices across periods need not be related at all.
