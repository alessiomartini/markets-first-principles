---
term: "Permanent impact"
aliases: ["permanent price impact"]
track: 2
status: written
---

The portion of a trade's **market impact** that does not decay: the price stays moved after the order finishes and the book refills. Conventionally it is interpreted as the information the market inferred from your trading — if you were buying, perhaps you knew something. How much impact is genuinely permanent is one of the honestly unsettled questions in the field, because it is hard to distinguish permanent impact from a very slowly decaying transient one with any finite sample.
