term: "Perpetual future"
aliases: ["perpetual swap", "perp", "perpetuals"]

A derivative that tracks an index like a futures contract but never expires. Because there is no delivery date to tie it to spot, a periodic funding payment between longs and shorts keeps the price close to the index. Proposed by Robert Shiller in 1993 and popularised in crypto by BitMEX from 2016, it accounts for a large share of crypto trading volume, with margin, liquidation and an insurance fund run by the exchange.
