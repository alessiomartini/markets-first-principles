---
term: "Phillips curve"
aliases: ["expectations-augmented Phillips curve"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The relation between inflation and slack in the economy, measured by unemployment or by the **output gap**. It began as an observed negative correlation between wage growth and unemployment in British data; in its modern form, inflation depends on expected inflation, on slack, and on supply shocks. The slope, meaning how much inflation moves for a given change in slack, is the central unknown: it has changed over time and across countries, and in some periods it has appeared nearly flat. It is a relation that a central bank cannot treat as a menu, since the position of the curve moves with **inflation expectations**.
