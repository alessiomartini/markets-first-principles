---
term: "P&L attribution"
aliases: ["performance attribution", "PnL attribution", "return attribution"]
track: 7
status: written
---

Decomposing the profit and loss of a period into its sources: the contribution of each factor (exposure at the start times the factor's return), the specific return, which is the closest thing to alpha, and costs. It shows whether money was made by the idea, by an unintended exposure or by luck, and checks that a book said to be neutral is in fact neutral.
