term: "Policy rate"

The short-term interest rate that a central bank sets, or steers by its operations, as its main instrument: the federal funds target range in the US, the deposit facility rate in the euro area, Bank Rate in the UK. It anchors the whole structure of short-term borrowing costs, since banks lend to each other and to customers at rates tied to it, and it influences longer yields through expectations of where it will go. When it cannot be lowered further, central banks turn to other tools such as **quantitative easing**. See **monetary transmission**.
