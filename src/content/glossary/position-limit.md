---
term: "Position limit"
aliases: ["position limits", "speculative position limit"]
track: 6
status: written
---

A cap on the number of contracts a trader may hold in a derivative, set by an exchange or regulator, mostly for commodities. It exists to stop one participant from cornering the deliverable supply, as the Hunt brothers tried to do in silver in 1979 and 1980. Limits are usually tightest in the spot month, when delivery is near and the deliverable supply is smallest. It differs from margin, which is collateral and not a cap on size.
