---
term: "Position sizing"
aliases: ["sizing", "bet sizing"]
track: 4
status: written
---

The choice of how much capital, or how much risk, to put into each position, given a view on expected return and a limit on how bad the path may get. It is a separate question from whether a strategy has an edge: a positive expected return sized too large can still end in ruin, and one sized too small leaves the edge unused. Common frameworks are the **Kelly criterion** and its fractional versions, **volatility targeting**, and **risk budgeting**.
