The price paid for an option. It splits into **intrinsic value**, what the option would pay if exercised immediately, and **time value**, everything else — the compensation for the chance that things improve before **expiry**. An out-of-the-money option is pure time value, which is why it decays to nothing if the move never comes.

In the fund sense, the same word is the amount by which a fund share trades above its net asset value; see **Discount**.
