What a future payment is worth today, obtained by dividing it by the growth a riskless investment would have achieved over the same period. Present value is the single idea underneath every valuation in finance: an instrument is worth the sum of the present values of what it pays. The difficulty is never the arithmetic but the rate — choosing it embeds a judgement about both the time value of money and the risk that the payment does not arrive.
