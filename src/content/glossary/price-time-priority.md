---
term: "Price-time priority"
aliases: ["price/time priority", "FIFO matching"]
track: 2

The most common matching rule: among resting orders, the best price is served first, and among orders at the same price, the one that arrived first is served first. It is not the only rule — some venues allocate pro rata across all orders at a price, and some blend the two — and the choice has large consequences for behaviour. Price-time priority rewards speed, because being early in the queue is valuable; pro rata rewards size, because allocation is proportional to quantity displayed.
