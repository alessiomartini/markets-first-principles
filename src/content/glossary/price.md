The rate at which two things exchanged, at one moment, in one quantity, between two particular parties. The definite article does a lot of damage here: at any instant there is a best **bid**, a best **ask**, a last traded price, an official closing price, and whatever a model says the thing is worth, and these are all different numbers. "The price" in a sentence about markets almost always means one specific one of them, and the first useful habit is to ask which. A price is also not a property of the asset — it is a property of a transaction, which is why the price you can get depends on how much you want and how quickly.
