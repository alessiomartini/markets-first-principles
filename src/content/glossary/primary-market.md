---
term: "Primary market"
aliases: ["primary issuance", "new issue"]
track: 0
status: written
---

The market in which a security is created and sold by its issuer for the first time, and where the proceeds go to the issuer: an IPO, a bond auction, a capital raise. This is the only point at which trading the security actually transfers money to the entity that issued it. Everything afterwards is the **secondary market**, where the security changes hands between investors and the issuer receives nothing. The distinction matters more than it sounds: arguments about whether markets "fund the real economy" are almost always arguments about which of these two is being talked about.
