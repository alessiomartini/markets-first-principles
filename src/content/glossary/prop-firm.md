A firm trading its own capital, with no clients and no outside money. Modern electronic prop firms are the dominant **market maker**s in listed equities, futures and options: small headcount, extreme investment in latency, and a business model of capturing the **bid-ask spread** millions of times rather than taking directional views. Having no clients means no redemption risk, which lets them run strategies that a fund answering to investors could not sustain through a bad quarter.
