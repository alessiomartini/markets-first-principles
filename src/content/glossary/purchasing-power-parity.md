---
term: "Purchasing power parity"
aliases: ["PPP"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The idea that a basket of goods should cost the same in two countries once converted at the exchange rate, so that exchange rates move with relative price levels. It works as a long-run tendency, with deviations lasting years, because many goods are not traded, transport costs and tariffs matter, and baskets differ. Not a guide to short-run moves.
