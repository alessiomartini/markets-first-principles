aliases: ["put", "puts"]

status: written
---

The right, without the obligation, to sell the **underlying** at the **strike**. A put is insurance: it pays when the asset falls, and its price behaves like a premium. Index puts trade persistently expensive relative to what subsequent realised moves justify, which is usually read as the market paying for protection against exactly the scenario in which everything else in a portfolio is failing at once.
