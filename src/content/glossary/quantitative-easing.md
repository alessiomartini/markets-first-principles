---
term: "Quantitative easing"
aliases: ["QE", "large-scale asset purchases", "LSAP"]
track: 5
status: written
---

A central bank buying large amounts of long-dated bonds with newly created reserves, used when the **policy rate** is already near zero and cannot be cut further. The purchases raise bond prices, lower long yields and the **term premium**, and expand the **monetary base**; the intended effect is to make borrowing cheaper across the curve and to push investors into other assets. How much of the effect comes from the purchases themselves and how much from signalling that rates will stay low is disputed. Reversing it, *quantitative tightening*, lets the holdings run off or sells them.
