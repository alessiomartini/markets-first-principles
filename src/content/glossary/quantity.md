How much of the thing is being traded — shares, contracts, notional, tonnes. Quantity is not an innocent second coordinate alongside price: the price available to you is a function of the quantity you want, and that function is steep. A quote is therefore only meaningful together with the size it is good for, which is why practitioners say "where are you for a million?" rather than "what is the price?". Traders usually say **size** for the quantity of an order and *clip* for the quantity of a single slice of it.
