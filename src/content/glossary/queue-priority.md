term: "Queue priority"
aliases: ["queue position", "priority"]

Your place in line among the orders resting at the same price. Because most venues match in **price-time priority**, an order that arrived earlier at your price gets filled before yours, and your position in that queue determines both whether you trade and, more subtly, *when*: orders at the back of the queue tend to fill only once the level is about to be exhausted, which is precisely when the price is about to move through you. Queue position is therefore an asset, and a large part of high-frequency trading is the business of acquiring and keeping it.
