---
term: "Quote-driven market"
aliases: ["quote driven", "dealer market"]
track: 0
status: written
---

A market where prices come from **dealer**s quoting two-sided prices on request, and customers trade against the dealer's own book rather than against each other. Bonds, swaps and much of **FX** work this way. The strength is that a dealer will show a price in size when no public book would, and will do so in an instrument too customised to standardise; the cost is that you see only the prices you are shown, so you cannot tell whether the quote is good without asking several dealers.
