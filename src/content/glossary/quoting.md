---
term: "Quoting"
aliases: ["quote", "two-sided quote"]
track: 2
status: written
---

Continuously posting both a **bid** and an **ask** at which you are willing to trade. Quoting is the act that supplies **liquidity**, and its economics are the whole of the market-making business: the quoted **bid-ask spread** must be wide enough that profits from uninformed traders cover losses to informed ones, and the quotes must be updated fast enough that they are not picked off when the market moves.
