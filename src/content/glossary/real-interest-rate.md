---
term: "Real interest rate"
aliases: ["real rate"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The interest rate adjusted for inflation, approximately the nominal rate minus inflation, or more exactly $(1+i)/(1+\pi) - 1$. It is the rate that matters for decisions about saving and investing because it measures the return in purchasing power rather than in money. The relevant inflation is the one expected over the life of the loan, so it can only be observed directly through inflation-linked bonds, whose yield is a real rate; otherwise it must be inferred from **inflation expectations**, which are uncertain.
