Regulation National Market System, the SEC rules of 2005 that tie the competing US equity venues into one market. Its Order Protection Rule forbids executing at a price worse than a better, automatically accessible quote on another venue, with the consolidated best bid and offer (NBBO) as the reference; other parts cap access fees and set a one-cent minimum quoting increment for most stocks. It produced a market of many venues that must respect each other's best prices.
