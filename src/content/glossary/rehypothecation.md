---
term: "Rehypothecation"
aliases: ["re-hypothecation", "reuse of collateral"]
track: 6
status: written
---

The reuse by a lender or prime broker of the collateral it received from a client, to secure its own borrowing. It lowers funding costs and lengthens the chain of claims on the same asset, and the client becomes an unsecured creditor to the extent that the asset is reused. The limits on it differ between jurisdictions, which proved important when Lehman Brothers' European arm failed in 2008.
