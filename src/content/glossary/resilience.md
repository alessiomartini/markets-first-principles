How quickly the **order book** refills after being consumed. It is the third and least measured dimension of **liquidity**, alongside the **bid-ask spread** and **depth**, and it is the one that determines whether a large order can be worked over time or must pay its full impact at once. A resilient book lets a patient trader split an order and let liquidity regenerate between slices; an unresilient one punishes every slice equally.
