---
term: "Retail flow"
aliases: ["retail", "retail investor", "retail order flow"]
track: 0
status: written
---

Orders originating from individual investors rather than institutions. Retail flow is prized by market makers because it is, on average, uninformed — it does not systematically predict the next price move, so quoting against it is profitable in a way that quoting against a hedge fund's execution algorithm is not. That is the entire economic basis of payment for order flow, and the reason retail orders are so often **internalisation** rather than sent to an exchange.
