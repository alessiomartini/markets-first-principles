Request for quote: the customer asks several **dealer**s for a price on a stated size, then trades with whichever is best. It is the price discovery mechanism of **quote-driven market**s, and it contains an inherent tension — asking more dealers gets a better sample of prices while revealing your intention to more people who can trade ahead of you. Deciding how many to ask is a real, unglamorous optimisation that professional desks think about carefully.
