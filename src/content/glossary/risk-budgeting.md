---
term: "Risk budgeting"
aliases: ["risk allocation", "risk parity"]
track: 4
status: written
---

Setting the size of each position in a portfolio so that its contribution to total portfolio risk is a chosen share of the whole, not its share of the capital. Equal capital in a calm asset and a volatile one takes almost all of its risk from the volatile one; a risk budget corrects for that. The contribution of a position depends on its volatility and on its correlation with the rest, so the budget needs a covariance estimate and inherits its errors. Allocating equal risk shares is often called *risk parity*.
