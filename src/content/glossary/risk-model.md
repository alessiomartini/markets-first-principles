---
term: "Risk model"
aliases: ["factor risk model", "Barra model", "covariance model"]
track: 7
status: written
---

A description of how asset returns move together in a form simple enough to estimate. Instead of a full covariance matrix, each asset's return is written as exposures to a few common factors (market, industries, styles such as size, value, momentum and volatility) plus a specific part, so that the risk of any portfolio can be predicted and split into factor and specific risk. It is an estimate and a simplification, and it fails when correlations change.
