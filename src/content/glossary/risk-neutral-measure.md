---
term: "Risk-neutral measure"
aliases: ["risk-neutral probability", "Q measure", "equivalent martingale measure"]
track: 4
status: written
---

A re-weighting of the probabilities of future outcomes under which the discounted price of every traded asset is a martingale, so that each asset is expected to earn the risk-free rate. Derivative prices are then expectations under this measure, discounted at the risk-free rate. The measure exists if and only if there is no **arbitrage**, and it is unique if markets are complete. It is a pricing device, not a forecast: it differs from the real-world measure by the market price of risk, so risk-neutral probabilities of bad outcomes are generally higher than the frequencies actually observed, reflecting what investors pay to be protected against them.
