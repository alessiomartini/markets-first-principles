---
term: "Risk of ruin"
aliases: ["probability of ruin", "gambler's ruin"]
track: 4
status: written
---

The probability that a strategy loses so much capital that it can no longer continue, or reaches a loss threshold set in advance. Even a strategy with a positive expected return has a risk of ruin above zero if each bet is large relative to the capital, because a run of losses can arrive before the edge shows up. Ruin is an absorbing state: nothing earned later can undo it, so averages over many strategies can hide it. This is why position sizing is chosen to keep the risk of ruin negligible, not only to maximise expected return. See **Kelly criterion**.
