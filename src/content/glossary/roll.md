---
term: "Roll"
aliases: ["rolling", "roll yield"]
track: 1
status: written
---

Closing a futures position in an expiring contract and reopening it in a later one, which anyone holding a long-term exposure through futures must do repeatedly. The roll has a cost or a benefit depending on the shape of the curve: rolling up a **contango** curve means repeatedly selling cheap and buying expensive. Over years this dominates the return of commodity index products, often overwhelming the move in the spot price itself.
