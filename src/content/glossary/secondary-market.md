The market in which an already-issued security is traded between investors, with no money reaching the issuer. Essentially all the trading anyone observes — every quote on a screen, every volume figure — is secondary. Its economic function is indirect but real: a security that can be resold easily is worth more at issuance, so a liquid secondary market lowers the cost of raising capital in the **primary market**. That link is the standard justification for caring about market quality at all.
