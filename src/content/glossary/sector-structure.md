term: "Sector structure"
aliases: ["sectors", "industry structure"]

---

The block structure in a **correlation matrix** arising because firms in the same industry share exposures. In the **eigenvalue spectrum** it appears as a small number of eigenvalues just above the noise bulk, beneath the dominant market mode. Its practical importance is that it is the part of the correlation structure which is both real and unstable — sector definitions drift, and a portfolio neutralised against yesterday's sectors is not neutral against today's.
