---
term: "Secured funding"
aliases: ["secured borrowing", "collateralised borrowing"]
track: 6
status: written
---

Borrowing against collateral, which the lender may keep or sell if the loan is not repaid. The repo is the standard form. Because the lender is protected, the rate depends little on who the borrower is and mostly on the supply of cash and the quality of the collateral, and it is usually below the rate on unsecured loans. Its weak point is the collateral: when its value or liquidity is in doubt, the haircut rises or the loan is not rolled.
