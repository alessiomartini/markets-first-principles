---
term: "Settlement fail"
aliases: ["fail to deliver", "fails"]
track: 6
status: written
---

A failure to deliver the asset, or less often the cash, on the settlement date. It is not a default: the trade stays valid and is usually completed a few days later, so the cost is the interest the buyer loses meanwhile, or a penalty. Fails are normally low and rise in stress or when a particular security is scarce, and they become more common when interest rates are near zero, since failing then costs almost nothing.
