A unit of ownership in a company, and the smallest indivisible piece of its **equity**. Shares carry a residual claim on assets and earnings, usually a vote, and no promise of anything. The count matters as much as the price: a company can raise the value of each share by buying some back, or dilute it by issuing more, without the business changing at all.
