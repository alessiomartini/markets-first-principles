---
term: "Simple return"
aliases: ["arithmetic return", "percentage return"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The proportional change in price, $R_t = S_t/S_{t-1} - 1$. It is the right quantity for reporting performance, because it is what actually happened to your money, and the wrong one for statistics, because simple returns compound rather than add: the two-period return is not the sum of the one-period returns. For small moves it agrees with the **log return** to first order; for the large moves that dominate risk it does not, and the difference is always in the same direction.
