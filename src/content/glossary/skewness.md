The normalised third moment, measuring asymmetry of a distribution. Equity index returns are negatively skewed: large falls are bigger and more frequent than large rises. This is not true of all assets — individual stocks are often positively skewed, and FX pairs are roughly symmetric — which is why skewness is a fact about specific markets rather than a universal stylised fact like **fat tails**.
