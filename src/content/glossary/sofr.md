The Secured Overnight Financing Rate, published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York since April 2018 and computed from overnight repo transactions backed by Treasury securities. It was chosen to replace USD LIBOR, which relied on bank estimates and not on transactions, and it is now the main reference for US dollar floating-rate loans and swaps. It stays close to the Fed's administered rates when cash is plentiful and spikes when it is not, as in September 2019.
