---
term: "Spot FX"
aliases: ["spot exchange rate", "spot rate"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The exchange rate for two currencies at the usual settlement date, normally two business days after the trade, quoted as units of the quote currency per unit of the base currency (EUR/USD of 1.10 means one euro costs 1.10 dollars). Trading is mostly over the counter between banks and dealers, around the clock on weekdays. Compare **forward FX**, which fixes today the rate for a later exchange.
