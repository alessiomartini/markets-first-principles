Trading for immediate delivery and payment, as opposed to a forward or futures contract that fixes today the terms of an exchange happening later. "Immediate" is a convention rather than a fact: spot equities settle a day or two later, spot FX usually two business days, spot crude on a schedule measured in weeks. The spot price is the reference from which every derivative on the same asset is priced, which is why disputes about what counts as *the* spot price are disputes about billions of notional.
