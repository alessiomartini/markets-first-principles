term: "Spread cost"
aliases: ["half-spread cost", "crossing the spread"]

The cost of crossing the bid-ask spread: a market buy pays the ask, above the midpoint by half the spread, and a later sale receives the bid, below it by the same amount, so a round trip costs the full spread. It is a few basis points per side for large liquid stocks and much more for small or illiquid ones. A strategy that posts passive orders can earn part of it, at the price of not being filled and of adverse selection.
