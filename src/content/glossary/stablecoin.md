A token designed to hold a fixed value, usually one US dollar. Most are backed by reserves of cash, deposits and Treasury bills that the holder can redeem; others by crypto collateral or an algorithm. Stablecoins are the cash of the crypto markets, used as collateral and as the quoting unit, and they share the weakness of money market funds: doubt about the reserves causes a run, as with TerraUSD in 2022 and the brief fall of USDC in March 2023.
