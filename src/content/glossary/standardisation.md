term: "Standardisation"
aliases: ["standardised", "contract specification"]

The deliberate act of defining a contract so precisely that its units become **fungible**: this grade, this quantity, this delivery location, this month. Exchanges standardise because it is the precondition for concentrating trading interest in one place — you cannot have a central order book in a contract every buyer must inspect individually. The trade-off is that a standard contract rarely matches anyone's exact exposure, which is what leaves **basis risk** behind and what keeps the customised over-the-counter market alive alongside it.
