---
term: "Steepening"
aliases: ["curve steepening", "steepener"]
track: 5
status: written
---

A widening of the gap between long-dated and short-dated yields on the yield curve. A *bull* steepening comes from short yields falling faster than long ones, typically when the central bank cuts; a *bear* steepening from long yields rising faster, as when growth, inflation or supply of bonds is expected to rise. Which kind it is matters for the interpretation, and the same label covers very different macroeconomic stories. It is the opposite of *flattening*, and a steepener is also the name of the trade that profits from it.
