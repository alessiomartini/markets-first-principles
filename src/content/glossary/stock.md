In economics, a quantity measured at a point in time: the capital in an economy, the amount of government debt, the money supply, a household's wealth. It is the accumulation of past **flows** and has no time dimension of its own. Not to be confused with a stock in the sense of a share of a company (see **equity**); the two uses share a word and nothing else.
