---
term: "Stress test"
aliases: ["scenario analysis"]
track: 4
status: written
---

An estimate of the loss a portfolio would suffer under a specified adverse scenario, such as a historical episode replayed on today's positions or a hypothetical shock to prices, rates and volatility. Unlike **value at risk**, which comes from a statistical model of the distribution of returns, a stress test makes no claim about how likely the scenario is. Its purpose is to show what the portfolio is exposed to when the statistical assumptions fail, for example when correlations jump to one. A stress test is only as informative as the scenarios chosen, and the scenario that matters most is often the one not imagined.
