---
term: "Survivorship bias"
aliases: ["survivor bias", "delisting bias"]
track: 7
status: written
---

The bias that arises when a dataset contains only the assets that survived to the end of the sample, so that companies and funds that failed, were delisted or were merged away are missing. A backtest of buying cheap stocks then excludes those that went bankrupt, and average fund performance looks better than what investors received. Brown, Goetzmann, Ibbotson and Ross (1992) and Elton, Gruber and Blake (1996) measured it for funds.
