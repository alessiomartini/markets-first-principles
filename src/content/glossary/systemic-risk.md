---
term: "Systemic risk"
aliases: ["contagion"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The risk that the failure or distress of one part of the financial system spreads to the rest, through common exposures, funding links or fire sales, so that the total damage is much larger than the first loss. It is a property of the network of balance sheets and not of any single firm, which is why it is hard to price and why regulators address it directly.
