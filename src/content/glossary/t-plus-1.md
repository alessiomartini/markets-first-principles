Settlement one business day after the trade date T. The settlement cycle is the lag between agreeing a trade and exchanging the asset and cash; the US moved from T+3 to T+2 in 2017 and to T+1 in May 2024. A shorter cycle lowers the exposure to a counterparty failing and the margin needed to cover it, at the price of less time to correct errors, fund currency conversions and recall lent securities.
