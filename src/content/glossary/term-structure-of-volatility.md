How **implied volatility** varies with the **expiry** of the option, at a fixed moneyness such as at the money. When near-term uncertainty is low relative to the long run the curve slopes upward; when markets are stressed, short-dated volatility jumps above long-dated volatility and the curve is inverted. The shape reflects mean reversion in volatility, scheduled events such as earnings or central bank meetings, and the premium demanded for uncertainty over longer horizons. Together with the **volatility smile** across strikes, it makes up the **volatility surface**.
