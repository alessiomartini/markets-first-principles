The part of an option's **premium** above its **intrinsic value** — what you pay for the possibility that the price moves your way before **expiry**. It rises with **volatility** and with time remaining, and it decays to exactly zero at expiry. Time value is the quantity an option seller is harvesting and an option buyer is renting, and the entire volatility-surface apparatus of Track 4 exists to price it.
