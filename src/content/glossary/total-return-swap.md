---
term: "Total return swap"
aliases: ["TRS"]
track: 1
status: written
---

A swap exchanging the entire economic return of an asset — price change plus income — for a financing rate. It lets one side hold an exposure without owning the asset, and the other side earn a spread on its balance sheet. Total return swaps are how leverage is often obtained discreetly, which is why they appear in post-mortems of large blowups.
