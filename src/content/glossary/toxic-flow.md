---
term: "Toxic flow"
aliases: ["toxicity", "informed flow"]
track: 2
status: written
---

Order flow that systematically predicts the next price move, and therefore loses money for whoever takes the other side. The term is descriptive rather than moral: a fund executing a well-researched position is producing toxic flow simply by being right. Market makers spend considerable effort classifying flow by toxicity and quoting different spreads to different sources, which is exactly what **internalisation** and payment for order flow industrialise.
