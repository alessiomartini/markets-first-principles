---

Volume traded relative to the amount outstanding, usually expressed per year. It measures how often the whole float changes hands, and it is the natural way to compare trading activity across assets of very different sizes. High turnover is not the same as high **liquidity**: a market can churn enormously in small size while offering no **depth** for anyone who actually needs to move a position.
