Time-weighted average price: a benchmark, and the execution schedule that targets it, in which the parent order is sliced evenly through the trading period regardless of volume. It is the simplest possible schedule and its weakness is exactly that — it trades the same amount in a thin hour as in a busy one, so it pays more **market impact** per share when the market is least able to absorb it.
