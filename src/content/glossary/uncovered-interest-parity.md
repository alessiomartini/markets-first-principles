---
term: "Uncovered interest parity"
aliases: ["UIP"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The condition that the currency with the higher interest rate is expected to depreciate by the interest differential, so that borrowing the cheap currency and lending the dear one earns nothing on average. It is a statement about expectations, not arbitrage, and fails empirically: high-yield currencies have tended to depreciate less than the differential, giving the **carry trade** a positive average return.
