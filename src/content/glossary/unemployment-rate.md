---
term: "Unemployment rate"
aliases: ["jobless rate"]
track: 5
status: written
---

The share of the labour force, meaning people who are working or actively looking for work, that is without a job. It comes from a household survey, not from benefit claims, and a person who has stopped looking drops out of the labour force and out of the rate. So it can fall because people find jobs or because they give up, which is why it is read together with the *participation rate* and the employment-to-population ratio. It is a measure of slack in the labour market, and a lagging one: it typically turns after output has already turned.
