---

Borrowing on the borrower's credit alone: deposits, interbank loans, commercial paper, certificates of deposit. The lender is exposed to the borrower's default, so the rate carries a credit premium and varies by borrower. The gap between unsecured and secured rates is a simple gauge of distrust in the banking system, and it widened sharply in 2007 to 2009.
