term: "Value at risk"
aliases: ["VaR"]

---

A quantile of the loss distribution: the loss that will not be exceeded with probability $p$ over a stated horizon. "One-day 99% VaR of £10m" means that on 1 day in 100 the loss should exceed £10m — a statement that is only meaningful with both the horizon and the confidence attached. VaR is the regulatory and desk-level lingua franca of risk, and its well-known defect is that it says nothing whatever about how bad the exceedances are, which is precisely the quantity that fat tails make dangerous.
