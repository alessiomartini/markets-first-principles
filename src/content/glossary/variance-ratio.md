---
term: "Variance ratio"
aliases: ["signature plot", "VR test"]
track: 3
status: written
---

The ratio of the variance of returns measured over a long horizon to the variance over a short one, scaled so that a memoryless random walk gives one. Plotted against lag it is called a *signature plot*, and its shape is diagnostic: values below one indicate mean reversion, above one trending. Real markets show a characteristic bend at very short lags caused by **bid-ask spread** bouncing rather than by any economic mechanism.
