The sensitivity of an option's price to a small change in the volatility of the **underlying**, $\partial V / \partial \sigma$. It is positive for both calls and puts, because more volatility raises the chance of a large payoff while the loss is capped at the premium. Vega is largest for options at the money and with a long time to **expiry**, and it shrinks as expiry approaches. It is the exposure a trader takes when betting on **implied volatility** itself rather than on the direction of the underlying. Despite the name, vega is not a Greek letter.
