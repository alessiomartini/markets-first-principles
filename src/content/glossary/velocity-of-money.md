---
term: "Velocity of money"
aliases: ["money velocity"]
track: 5
status: written
---

How often, on average, a unit of money is used to pay for final output in a period: nominal GDP divided by the money stock. It is the term that turns the quantity equation $MV = PY$ from an identity into a theory. If velocity were constant, money growth would pass one-for-one into nominal income; in practice velocity moves with interest rates, payment technology and confidence, and it fell sharply after 2008, which is one reason large increases in the **monetary base** did not produce the inflation some expected.
