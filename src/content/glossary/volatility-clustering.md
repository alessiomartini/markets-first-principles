term: "Volatility clustering"
aliases: ["clustered volatility", "volatility persistence"]
track: 3
status: written
physics: "Intermittency in the strict turbulence sense: activity concentrated in bursts, generated by a multiplicative cascade across scales rather than by independent shocks."
---

The tendency for large moves to be followed by large moves and quiet periods by quiet periods, regardless of direction. It is the most robust stylised fact about prices after **fat tails**, and the two are related: a distribution built by mixing calm and violent regimes has heavier tails than either regime alone. What makes clustering remarkable is its persistence — the autocorrelation of absolute returns decays as a power law over months, so there is no horizon at which volatility forgets, which is the property that separates it from anything an exponential model can generate.
