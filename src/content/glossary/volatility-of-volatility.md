The variability of **volatility** itself. Because volatility is not constant, it has its own dynamics and its own risk, and that second-order quantity is directly traded through options on volatility indices. It is also the parameter that stochastic volatility models add to Black–Scholes, and the one that generates a **volatility smile**: uncertainty about future variance fattens the tails of the terminal distribution.
