---
term: "Volatility smile"
aliases: ["smile", "volatility skew", "skew"]
track: 4
status: written
---

The shape traced by **implied volatility** when plotted against strike for a single expiry. Black-Scholes assumes one volatility for all strikes, so a flat line; the market prices a curve, typically higher for out-of-the-money puts than for calls in equity indices, which is called the *skew* rather than a smile. The shape is not a failure of arbitrage but the market's correction to the model: it encodes **fat tails** and the **panic asymmetry** that Black-Scholes leaves out, restated in the only language options have.
