Volume-weighted average price: the average price of all trades in a period, weighted by size, and the most common benchmark against which institutional execution is judged. A VWAP schedule trades in proportion to expected volume, which is the natural improvement on **TWAP**. Being benchmarked to VWAP creates its own behaviour: a broker measured this way is incentivised to track the market rather than to trade well, and will happily buy into a rising price because the benchmark rises with it.
