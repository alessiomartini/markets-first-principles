The yields on bonds of equal credit quality plotted against their maturity, usually for government debt from a few months to thirty years. It is built from the **zero-coupon curve**, so each point is the annualised return from lending for exactly that horizon. Its shape combines the expected path of short rates with a **term premium**, and is described by its level, its slope and its curvature. *Steepening*, *flattening* and *curve inversion* are changes in that shape.
