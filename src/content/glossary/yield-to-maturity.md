---
term: "Yield to maturity"
aliases: ["YTM", "yield", "redemption yield"]
track: 1
status: written
---

The single discount rate which, applied to all a bond's remaining cash flows, reproduces its current price. It is a *summary statistic*, not a forecast and not a rate anyone pays: it assumes every coupon is reinvested at that same rate, which will not happen. Yield is useful because it compresses a whole schedule of payments into one comparable number, and it is misleading for exactly the same reason.
